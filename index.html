BEACH RUSH

H-HOUR. THE DOORS ARE OPENING.

Run the beach with 200 others. Reach the sea wall, plant the Bangalore torpedo, blow the wall, destroy the three bunkers and take the trench.

WASD move   Shift sprint   Mouse aim   Click fire   Right mouse aim down sights   1 M1 Garand   2 Colt   R reload   E plant Bangalore   C crouch   Enter yell CHARGE

Everyone dives for cover when they land. Press Enter to yell CHARGE and push the line up the beach. Hug the sea wall to be safe from the bunkers, or drop into a crater for partial cover. Then blow the wall, climb the hill and take the bunkers out. Grab medkits on the way.

The crosshair stays in the middle of the screen and the mouse turns your view. Click the game to capture the mouse. The arrow keys also turn, and Space also fires.
